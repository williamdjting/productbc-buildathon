## TL;DR
- **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)** helps your page get selected as a **direct answer** (snippets, assistants, voice).
- **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** helps your brand/content get **mentioned accurately** in **AI-generated** responses.
- You usually need both: **AEO for extractable answers**, **GEO for trusted inclusion**.

## Why this matters now
People don’t just “search and click” anymore. They ask a question and get an answer instantly—often without visiting a site. If your content isn’t structured for extraction (AEO) or trusted enough to be referenced (GEO), you can lose visibility even if your traditional SEO is solid.

## What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)?
**AEO** is the practice of making content easy for answer systems to *pull a clean, correct response*.

### Where AEO shows up
- Google featured snippets / “People also ask”
- Bing answers
- AI assistants that quote a paragraph
- Voice assistants reading a single response aloud

### AEO checklist (quick)
- Write a **one-paragraph answer** near the top (40–80 words)
- Use **question-style headings** (e.g., “What is AEO?”)
- Add **FAQ / HowTo schema** where appropriate
- Keep definitions consistent (avoid swapping terms)
- Make pages fast, readable, and accessible

## What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?
**GEO** is the practice of improving the chances that generative AI will *include you in its synthesized answer*—and represent you correctly.

### Where GEO shows up
- “Best tools for X” answers in AI chat
- Summaries that cite multiple sources
- Recommendations that compare brands/vendors

### GEO checklist (quick)
- Publish **original insights** (data, benchmarks, frameworks)
- Strengthen **E‑E‑A‑T** signals (author bios, references, credentials)
- Ensure **entity consistency** across the web (name, product, description)
- Create “quotable blocks”: definitions, pros/cons, comparisons, tables
- Earn citations/mentions from reputable sources

## AEO vs GEO: the difference in one sentence
- **AEO**: “Make my page the best *extractable answer*.”
- **GEO**: “Make my brand a *trusted ingredient* in AI’s final response.”

## A simple example (same topic, two optimizations)

### AEO-style snippet (extractable)
**AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)** is optimizing content so search engines and assistants can extract a clear, direct answer to a question, often shown as snippets, voice responses, or AI citations.

### GEO-style positioning (mention-worthy)
If you want AI to recommend your product, publish transparent comparisons, real performance data, and clear positioning statements so the model can accurately place you among alternatives.

## 7-day starter plan
1. Pick 10 high-intent questions your audience asks.
2. For each page, add a 60-word direct answer under the H1.
3. Add 3–5 FAQs and mark them up with schema.
4. Add author info + references to primary sources.
5. Publish one “unique asset” (benchmark, dataset, or framework).
6. Update product/about pages to be consistent across directories/social.
7. Track: snippet wins (AEO) and AI mentions (GEO) over time.

## Final takeaway
Traditional SEO still matters, but **answers are the new homepage**. Use **AEO** to become the cleanest answer, and **GEO** to become the most trusted reference.
